HOW PAYMENT AND CREDITING WORK

Clear steps. Manual payment verification.

1

Choose a plan

Compare credit count, source file limit, and the clip allowance.

2

Pay on Selar

Use the Selar product page or scan the plan’s QR code when available. If you do not have a Selar account, create one before checkout.

Open Selar’s sign-up guide ↗
3

Submit your reference

Return to Kraftaa, select “I have made payment,” and provide the Selar transaction reference.

4

We verify and credit

An admin checks Selar or the connected bank account. Approved payments automatically add the credits shown on the purchased plan.

Credits remain pending until the payment is manually verified. Do not share your Selar password or banking details with Kraftaa.
